Roofline: Attention & FFN on L40S

Analytical arithmetic intensity (FLOPs/byte) for Llama-3-8B's core ops across sequence lengths, plotted against the L40S's bf16 roofline (362 TFLOPS peak, 864 GB/s bandwidth, ridge point at 419 FLOPs/byte). Each point sits at its op's ceiling performance given its arithmetic intensity — below the ridge, that ceiling is set by memory bandwidth; above it, by compute.

Decode-phase attention never leaves the memory-bound region — its arithmetic intensity saturates around 4 FLOPs/byte regardless of how long the KV-cache grows (both its FLOPs and its bytes scale linearly with cache length, so the ratio converges). Every other op starts memory-bound at short sequence lengths but crosses into compute-bound territory once the batched sequence length reaches a few hundred to a couple thousand tokens — FFN crosses first (~512 tokens), matching the classic result that MLP/FFN layers are the most "GEMM-like" (easiest to make compute-bound) part of a transformer.
Full data: roofline_data.csv / roofline_data.json, generated by compute_roofline.py — a pure analytical FLOP/byte model (no GPU run required), cross-checked against the empirical LM-head-GEMV finding in ../report.md (batch=1 GEMV there is the same memory-bound regime as decode-phase attention/projections here).
