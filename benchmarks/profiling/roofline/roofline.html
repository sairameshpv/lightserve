Roofline: Attention & FFN on L40S

Analytical arithmetic intensity (FLOPs/byte) for Llama-3-8B's core ops across sequence lengths, plotted against the L40S's bf16 roofline (362 TFLOPS peak, 864 GB/s bandwidth, ridge point at 419 FLOPs/byte). Filled dots sit at each op's theoretical ceiling given its arithmetic intensity; open rings are the same shapes measured on a real L40S via CUDA-event timing — the gap between the two is real-world GEMM/kernel-launch efficiency, not modeling error.

Decode-phase attention never leaves the memory-bound region — its arithmetic intensity saturates around 4 FLOPs/byte regardless of how long the KV-cache grows (both its FLOPs and its bytes scale linearly with cache length, so the ratio converges). Every other op starts memory-bound at short sequence lengths but crosses into compute-bound territory once the batched sequence length reaches a few hundred to a couple thousand tokens — FFN crosses first (~512 tokens), matching the classic result that MLP/FFN layers are the most "GEMM-like" (easiest to make compute-bound) part of a transformer.

Measured on the real L40S, the ceiling itself is optimistic. Peak achieved was 350.8 TFLOPS (attention prefill, S=2048) — 97% of the 362 TFLOPS spec, and the closest anything gets. But once FFN crosses into "compute-bound" territory its real efficiency drops, from ~81% of ceiling at S=128 to just 42% at S=8192 — being compute-bound by arithmetic intensity doesn't guarantee the GEMM actually reaches peak. At the opposite end, S=1–32 attention/QKVO shapes achieve only 0.1–15% of their (already tiny) memory-bound ceiling: fixed per-kernel launch overhead dominates when there's almost no work to hide it behind, a latency-bound regime the classic roofline model doesn't capture. And decode attention at S=8192 measures 1156 GB/s — above the 864 GB/s HBM spec, because its ~34MB of K/V fits inside the L40S's L2 cache.
Analytical data: roofline_data.csv / roofline_data.json, generated by compute_roofline.py — a pure analytical FLOP/byte model (no GPU run required). Measured data: roofline_measured.json (raw CUDA-event timings from measure_roofline.py, run inside the vllm-openai container on the actual vllm-node-0 L40S) merged against the model in roofline_comparison.json via compare_measured.py. Cross-checked against the empirical LM-head-GEMV finding in ../report.md (batch=1 GEMV there is the same memory-bound regime as decode-phase attention/projections here).
