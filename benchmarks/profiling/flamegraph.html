Prefill vs Decode: Where the Milliseconds Go

GPU kernel time for two isolated probe requests against the vLLM server (Llama-3-8B, L40S, CUDA graphs on) — prefill: 140→1 tokens · decode: 2→200 tokens, 200 steps. Source: Nsight Systems (nsys) kernel trace.

Same kernel dominates both phases. The LM-head vocab-logits GEMV (gemvx::kernel) costs ~1.45ms per generation step whether it's the one prefill step or any one of the 200 decode steps — at batch size 1 it's a memory-bandwidth-bound matrix–vector product over the full 128k-token vocabulary, not compute-bound attention. Attention itself is only 15% of prefill time and doesn't crack decode's top 5.
